## Platform

web

## Users

Primary: brand marketers and advertisers (confirmed in the init interview), deciding whether to talk to GrainzMedia about performance campaigns, and about reaching audiences in physical retail environments.

Second: property owners and asset managers of shopping malls, mixed-use developments and multi-property portfolios, deciding whether to build and commercialise a retail media network. The Retail Media offer is written for them.

Both arrive to decide whether to send an enquiry. The Retail Media and Performance Marketing offers can be engaged independently or connected.

## Product Purpose

GrainzMedia's site presents a Singapore-based retail media commercialisation, experience and performance marketing company serving Singapore and Asia. It exists to turn visits into qualified enquiries: from brands that want measurable growth, and from property owners that want to become media owners, data businesses and commerce platforms.

Success is an enquiry sent through the contact form (topic: Retail Media, Performance Marketing, Integrated campaign, or Something else).

Company framing from the site copy:
- Vision: help retail properties evolve into media owners, data businesses and commerce platforms.
- Mission: build valuable audience connections and turn them into measurable commercial action.
- Core idea: "From attention to action", connecting the environments where discovery happens with the marketing that drives action.

## Positioning

## Product Principles

1. Lead with the marketer's outcome. The brand's question is "what action will this drive?", so frame every offer around a business result, not a service inventory.
2. Show the connection, not two separate pitches. Retail media and performance marketing stay independently buyable, but the unified physical-to-digital journey is what a neighbouring agency can't claim.
3. Claim only what's true. With no proof on hand, credibility comes from precise language, a clear process and honest "coming soon" states, never from invented numbers or logos.
4. Make the enquiry effortless. Every page should end in a low-friction, topic-aware route to contact.
5. Serve both audiences without blurring them. Brand marketers are primary. Property owners get a complete dedicated path, clearly addressed to them.
